Transformation succeeds when leaders, practitioners, and the people affected by change can work from the same understanding of the problem.

With leaders

I help leaders move beyond abstract AI ambition to concrete choices: what should change, where value can be created, what risks matter, and how progress should be judged.

With builders

I work with technical teams to connect models, data, and platforms to a genuine use case. The product must fit the workflow and make sense to the people it serves.

With partners

Research institutions, community organizations, employers, clients, and investors may enter from different directions. I value collaborations that combine rigorous thinking with the commitment to build, test, and learn.
