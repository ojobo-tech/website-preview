Consulting, engineering, and business intelligence

My foundation combines a Bachelor of Engineering and an MBA in Nigeria with work in management consulting, technology consulting, training, and business intelligence.

Data science, research, and the public interest

I earned an MS in Data Science at Rutgers University and moved further into machine learning, applied research, and public-interest technology.

I conceived and led CHEDVI, a live public-facing health-equity data platform for Camden. My published work includes the Rutgers State AI Preparedness Project, which I presented with colleagues at the 2025 AAAS Annual Meeting, and a systematic review of explainable AI in healthcare in Algorithms.

AI transformation and adoption

The work is both strategic and practical. CHEDVI shows the platform side of this practice; my transformation framework shows how I approach the organizational side.

Today I connect that background to the questions leaders face: which problem is worth solving, which use case creates value, how work and decisions should change, what capability people need, and what practical solution should be built.
