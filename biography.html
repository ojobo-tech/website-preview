Foundations

I began with engineering, analytics, and business intelligence: building ways to understand operational information and use it to make better decisions. That foundation still shapes how I approach AI—through evidence, systems, and the needs of the people using them.

Research and public interest

My work expanded into data science, visualization, and research across healthcare, public policy, community development, and AI readiness. Those settings reinforced a practical lesson: a technically impressive model is not enough if its output is not understandable or usable.

Current work

Today I focus on AI transformation: helping organizations identify the right problems, redesign work, build human capability, and develop platforms that support action. CHEDVI is one example of the platform side of this practice.
