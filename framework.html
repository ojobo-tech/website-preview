Choosing a use case

A use case is not a general desire to “use AI.” It is a specific application connected to a real problem and a recognizable process. The question is whether the proposed technology improves the outcome, and whether the organization can support it responsibly.

Making change last

Skills mean more than a one-off training session. People need understanding, capability, and confidence. Culture means the organization supports the experimentation and adaptation needed to make transformation worthwhile and sustainable.
